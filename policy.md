# Accounts payable: invoice matching policy

1. An invoice must reference a purchase order raised on the same vendor. If the
   PO is missing or belongs to another vendor, escalate.
2. Price variance: if the invoice total is within 2% of the PO total and the
   difference is under Rs 5,000, approve. Anything above that is escalated.
3. Quantity: we never pay for more units than were ordered. Reject and ask the
   vendor for a corrected invoice. Billing fewer units than ordered is fine
   (partial delivery).
4. Vendors on hold: no payments. Escalate to the vendor manager.
5. Duplicates: if another invoice from the same vendor has the same invoice
   number, reject the later one.
6. If none of the above covers the case, escalate. Do not guess.
